HACKATHON PROJECT

Novel signal processing concept proposed for ISRO astronomical data analysis.

🛰️ BHARATIYA ANTARIKSH HACKATHON 2026  |  ISRO / HACK2SKILL

AI-enabled Detection of Exoplanets from Noisy Astronomical Light Curves

National Space Hackathon  ·  Deep Space Signal Analysis

Project Concept: "Exo-Acoustics"

⚠️ The Challenge

Astronomical photometric light curves captured by space telescopes suffer from deep instrumentation noise and stellar variability, making faint exoplanetary transit dips difficult to detect with traditional threshold methods.

🎵 The "Exo-Acoustics" Approach

Convert photometric time-series light curve curves into audio/waveform representations, generate high-resolution spectrograms, and treat periodic transit signatures as acoustic-spectral anomalies.

🤖 AI / Deep Learning Direction

Investigate Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) and LSTM recurrent models trained directly on spectrogram representations to classify transit signals and separate real planetary candidates from false positives.

Team Structure (4 Members): 2 CSE (Data Science) Students — Data transformation, ML pipelines, and spectrogram generation 2 EEE Students — Signal analysis, wave modeling, and sensor noise characteristics
